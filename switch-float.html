Switch #5: Float Switch

Gravity & buoyancy tilted liquid-level automatic controller

Description & Definition

A Float Switch is an electromechanical level sensor designed to detect the presence or depth of liquid inside a tank, sump, or vessel. Utilizing Archimedes' principle of buoyancy, the float rising or falling tilts an internal mechanical microswitch or steel ball to automatically start/stop water pumps and prevent pump dry-running.

Tilt Ball & Microswitch Operating Principle

Low Water Level (Tilted Down): The buoy hangs vertically under gravity. Internal steel ball rolls backward, releasing the microswitch lever. NC contact closes to trigger the motor pump ON.
High Water Level (Tilted Up): Buoyant force floats the capsule upward. The internal steel ball rolls forward under gravity, pressing the microswitch. NO contact opens to turn pump OFF (prevents overflow).
Enclosure Protection IP68 Waterproof Sealed
Switch Rating 10A - 16A @ 250V AC

Key Components & Internal Mechanics

  • •
    Hermetically Sealed Polypropylene Buoy: Chemical-resistant, high-impact blow-molded plastic float body.
  • •
    Rolling Steel Ball & Mechanical Microswitch: A precision stainless steel ball that rolls along a molded internal track to trip the electrical contact lever when tilted.
  • •
    Neoprene Waterproof Flexible Cable: High-flex 3-core insulated cable (Common, NO, NC) entering the float through a molded waterproof strain relief gland.
  • •
    Adjustable Cable Counterweight: Heavy sliding weight clamped along the cable, serving as the pivot point for setting liquid level differential.

Practical Laboratory Applications

In Diploma Electrical & Automation labs, Float switches are utilized in motor control circuit experiments:

  • Automatic Overhead Tank Fill Circuit: Wired to DOL motor starter coil to start pump when tank is empty and stop when full.
  • Underground Sump Dry-Run Protection: Interlocked in series with overhead tank starter coil to prevent motor damage if the underground water source runs dry.
  • Industrial Sump Dewatering: Sump pumps wired in drainage pits to automatically evacuate accumulated wastewater.
  • PLC & Automation Input: Serves as digital input (DI) signal for PLC water treatment plant monitoring.
