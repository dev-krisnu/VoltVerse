Industrial Machine

Component Function

Integrated electromechanical machine converting direct current (DC) electrical power into continuous mechanical shaft rotation.

Working Principle & Purpose

Operates on Lorentz Force ($F = B \cdot I_a \cdot L$). Stationary field poles produce magnetic flux ($\Phi$). Current-carrying armature conductors cut this flux, producing continuous mechanical torque ($T$).
