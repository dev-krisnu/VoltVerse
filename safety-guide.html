The Universal Golden Rules of Electrical Safety

01.
Treat Every Wire as Live

Never assume a wire or equipment is dead or safe until you test it yourself with a certified voltage detector.

02.
Keep Dry and Isolated

Water conducts electricity easily. Always keep hands, floors, and equipment completely dry before operating switches.

03.
Never Overload Outlets

Plugging too many heavy appliances into one extension cord creates extreme heat and risks electrical fires.
