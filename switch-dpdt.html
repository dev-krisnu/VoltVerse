Switch #11: Double Pole Double Throw (DPDT)

6-Terminal Dual Line Reversing & Routing Switch

Operational Purpose

A Double Pole Double Throw (DPDT) switch consists of two independent Single Pole Double Throw (SPDT) mechanisms mechanically linked to operate via a single actuator handle. Featuring 6 distinct electrical terminals (2 center Common Poles and 4 Throw Contacts), a DPDT switch is the ultimate mechanical device for routing two inputs between two separate sets of output loads or reversing DC power polarity.

Terminal Matrix & Connection Logic

Position 1 (Throw A)

COM 1 ➔ Throw 1A

COM 2 ➔ Throw 2A

Position 2 (Throw B)

COM 1 ➔ Throw 1B

COM 2 ➔ Throw 2B

Terminal Count 6 Pins (2 COM, 4 Throws)
Contact Variations ON-ON / ON-OFF-ON

Mechanical Construction

  • •
    Center Common Terminals (Poles 1 & 2): Located in the middle row. These receive the primary input voltage or armature supply.
  • •
    Outer Throw Terminals (1A/2A & 1B/2B): Located on top and bottom rows. Used to route currents to load set A or load set B.
  • •
    Ganged Actuator Detent: Internal mechanical spring linkage ensures both pole blades change positions simultaneously with ZERO risk of cross-phase short circuits.

Practical DC Motor Reversing Circuit (H-Bridge)

Crossover Wiring Setup In Electrical Diploma Labs, a DPDT switch is used as a manual mechanical H-Bridge for DC Shunt/Permanent Magnet motors:
  1. Connect DC Power (+V and -V) to center COM 1 and COM 2.
  2. Wire Throw 1A and Throw 2B together to Motor Terminal (+)
  3. Cross-wire Throw 2A and Throw 1B together to Motor Terminal (-)
  4. Toggling the switch flips the DC voltage polarity across the motor armature, instantly reversing rotation (FORWARD ➔ REVERSE).
