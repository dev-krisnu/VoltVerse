Switch #13: Intermediate Switch

4-Terminal Crossover Switch for Multi-Way (3+ Location) Lighting

Purpose & Application

An Intermediate Switch (also known as a 4-way switch in North America) is a specialized 4-terminal mechanical switch used in building electrical installations. It enables a single lighting load to be independently turned ON or OFF from three or more separate locations when wired between two 2-Way (SPDT) switches.

Staircase & Long Corridors

Installed on middle floors of multi-story staircases or at intermediate points in long hallways to allow full control of overhead lamps from any floor level.

Large Master Bedrooms

Allows switching room lights from the entrance door, as well as both left and right bedside control panels.

Terminal Count 4 Terminals (L1, L2, L3, L4)
Switch Type Double Pole Crossover (DPDT internal)

Internal 4-Terminal Crossover Mechanics

  • •
    Position 1 (Straight-Through): Connects Terminal L1 directly to L3, and Terminal L2 directly to L4.
  • •
    Position 2 (Cross-Over): Swaps the internal links, connecting Terminal L1 across to L4, and Terminal L2 across to L3.
  • •
    Reversing Action: By reversing the active pair of strapper/traveler wires coming from the first two-way switch, it instantly flips the power state at the lamp regardless of the positions of all other switches in the loop.

Diploma Practical: Controlling 1 Lamp from 3 Locations

Wiring Rules for 3-Point Control
  1. First Location: Use a standard 2-Way (SPDT) Switch. Connect Phase (L) to COM terminal. Run 2 strapper wires from L1 & L2 to the Intermediate switch.
  2. Middle Location(s): Connect the 2 incoming strapper wires to input terminals of the Intermediate Switch. Connect 2 outgoing strappers to the next switch.
  3. End Location: Use a second 2-Way Switch. Connect incoming strappers to L1 & L2. Connect COM terminal to the Lamp Load.
