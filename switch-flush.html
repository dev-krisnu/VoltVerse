Switch #12: Surface & Flush Switch

Domestic Wall Wiring & Modular Box Switches (6A/16A, 240V AC)

Purpose & Classification

Surface and Flush switches are standard low-voltage AC domestic wall switches used in building wiring to control lighting, fans, and socket outlets. They are classified based on their installation enclosure method:

1. Surface Switch

Mounted directly onto the outer wall surface on a wooden/PVC junction box. Used in exposed surface conduit wiring (PVC/GI pipes) or retrofitting older installations.

2. Flush (Modular) Switch

Embedded inside a metal or PVC backbox recessed within the brickwork. Only the front faceplate and rocker lever remain flush with the wall surface for aesthetic, sleek architectural design.

Standard Rating 6A / 16A, 240V AC 50Hz
Material Body Polycarbonate (Flame Retardant)

Internal Mechanical Assembly

  • •
    Rocker Mechanism & Detent Spring: Operates on a spring-loaded pivot blade that snaps cleanly into position to prevent arcing when contacts close.
  • •
    Silver-Cadmium Contact Tips: High conductivity silver alloy contacts resist pitting and welding under inductive loads (fluorescent ballast, motors).
  • •
    Brass Screw Terminals: Dual captive screw terminals at the rear accept 1.5mm² to 2.5mm² copper wires for Phase Line input and Switched Phase output.

Diploma Practical: Single Lamp Control Wiring

Standard Connection Rules In Electrical Wiring Practical Labs:
  1. Phase Wire (Live): MUST always connect to the input terminal of the switch. Never switch the Neutral wire.
  2. Switched Phase (Load): Runs from the output switch terminal directly to the lamp holder.
  3. Neutral Wire: Runs directly from the distribution board/junction box to the lamp holder.

Standard IEC 60617 One-Way Wall Switch Schematic Symbol
