DC Series Motor Overview

Field Windings Connected in Series with Armature

Why is it named this way?

Named 'Series' because the field coils ($S_1\text{--}S_2$) are connected directly in series with the armature winding ($A_1\text{--}A_2$). The entire line current ($I_L$) flows through both the field and armature.

Coil Construction & Gauge

Constructed with very thick copper wire (low resistance $R_{se}$) and relatively few turns to carry the full load current without excessive voltage drop or overheating.
