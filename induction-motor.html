STATOR ASSEMBLY

Stator Core & Windings

Functional Overview

Outer stationary housing and laminated silicon steel core. Provides mechanical support, houses the 3-phase windings, and creates a low-reluctance path for magnetic flux.

CONSTRUCTION MATERIAL Cast Iron / Die-cast Aluminum
KEY SPECIFICATION Silicon Steel Laminations (0.5mm)

Engineering Features

  • External cooling fins maximize heat dissipation surface area.
  • Laminations reduce eddy current power losses significantly.
  • Holds 3-phase distributed windings spaced 120° electrical degrees apart.
Topology Comparison

Stator remains fundamentally identical for both Squirrel Cage and Wound Rotor induction motors.
