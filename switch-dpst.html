Switch #10: Double Pole Single Throw (DPST)

Dual-circuit master isolator (Switches Live & Neutral simultaneously)

Operational Purpose

A Double Pole Single Throw (DPST) switch controls two separate electrical circuits simultaneously through a single mechanical actuator handle. It has 4 terminal connections: 2 input poles (e.g., Live and Neutral) and 2 output throws. When turned OFF, it completely isolates both lines from the load, providing 100% electrical safety.

Terminal Configuration

Pole 1 (L1 ➔ L2)

Phase / Live Line Isolation

Pole 2 (N1 ➔ N2)

Neutral Line Isolation

Contact Arrangement 2 NO (Normally Open)
Safety Classification Double-Pole Disconnect

Key Components

  • •
    Common Actuator Lever / Rocker: Single mechanical bar connecting two isolated internal contact mechanisms to ensure ganged opening/closing.
  • •
    Dual Contact Blades: Parallel heavy-duty copper/brass conductive arms for Phase and Neutral lines.
  • •
    Insulated Dielectric Housing: High-temperature Bakelite/Polycarbonate casing maintaining complete electrical breakdown isolation between Pole 1 and Pole 2.

Laboratory Experiments & Safety Applications

230V AC Mains Appliance Main Disconnect Used on heavy domestic appliances (Water Heaters, Air Conditioners, Industrial Test Benches). Turning off a DPST switch guarantees that voltage cannot enter the load via neutral reversal or floating grounds.
Diploma Lab: Single Phase Induction Motor Starter Wire Line to Pole 1 COM and Neutral to Pole 2 COM. Connect the output terminals of both poles directly to the motor main winding to provide a full line-break isolation switch.
