Switch #6: Limit Switch

Heavy-duty roller-lever electromechanical position interlock switch

Definition & Operational Role

A Limit Switch is an electromechanical device actuated by the physical force or contact of a moving object, machine component, or conveyor workpiece. When an object strikes the actuator (such as a roller lever), it triggers internal contacts to alter an electrical control circuit, signaling arrival, end-of-travel, or safety interlocking.

Common Mechanical Actuator Types

Roller Lever Actuator: Uses a rotary arm with a steel roller wheel to absorb sliding friction from moving machine cams (as modeled in 3D viewport).
Push Plunger Actuator: Operates via direct linear perpendicular mechanical displacement (ideal for short stroke precision stopping).
Flexible Whisker / Coil Spring: Multi-directional flexible rod triggered by objects approaching from any arbitrary angle.
Protection Rating IP67 Oil/Dust Tight

Internal Mechanical Components

  • •
    Cast Metal Alloy Housing: Die-cast aluminum alloy box with heavy mounting flange holes and oil-resistant seals.
  • •
    Roller Lever & Shaft Assembly: Hardened steel wheel attached to a spring-loaded shaft that converts rotational displacement into linear pin pressing.
  • •
    Snap-Action Contact Block: High-speed internal spring mechanism that transfers electrical contacts rapidly regardless of how slow the machine physical contact moves.
  • •
    Threaded Conduit Entry Gland: ½" NPT or M20 bottom entry for armored cabling and sealing against industrial coolants.

Industrial & Lab Control Applications

In Diploma Electrical, PLC, and Industrial Automation practicals, limit switches form core safety interlocks:

  • Conveyor Automatic Stop / Reverse: Mounted at the end of a belt to de-energize the motor starter when a box reaches the end position.
  • Overhead Traveling Crane Safety Stop: Stops crane hoist and trolley motors before physical crash against structural end stops.
  • Elevator Floor Leveling: Senses cabin alignment at each building floor to signal brake engagement.
  • Machine Tool Guard Safety Interlock: Ensures CNC machine power is cut whenever access doors are opened.
