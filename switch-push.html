Switch #3: Push Button Switch

Momentary contact actuator with internal helical return spring

Description & Definition

A Push Button Switch is a momentary or latching mechanical switch actuated by applying downward finger pressure on a plunger. Once released, an internal compression spring automatically restores the plunger and contacts to their default state.

Contact Configurations (NO vs NC)

Normally Open (NO): Contacts remain open until pressed. Used primarily as START / MAKE push buttons.
Normally Closed (NC): Contacts remain closed until pressed. Used primarily as STOP / BREAK / EMERGENCY push buttons.
Operating Force 3.0N - 8.0N
Electrical Life 100,000+ Cycles

Internal Mechanical Assembly

  • •
    Color-Coded Plunger: Red (STOP), Green (START), Yellow (RE-SET) high-impact polycarbonate cap.
  • •
    Compression Helical Spring: Stainless steel return spring providing restoring mechanical force.
  • •
    Stationary & Movable Contacts: Silver-alloy contact bridge that shorts stationary terminals when depressed.
  • •
    Threaded Metal Bezel: Industrial 22mm / 30mm diameter panel-mount body with locknut.

Diploma Practical Laboratory Applications

In Diploma Electrical Laboratories, push buttons form the primary human-machine control interface:

  • Direct-On-Line (DOL) Starter: Green NO Start push button in parallel with contactor auxiliary holding contact.
  • Motor Reversing Station: Dual NO/NC interlocked push button control stations.
  • Emergency Stop Station: Mushroom-head twist-to-release NC push button in series with control circuit main line.
